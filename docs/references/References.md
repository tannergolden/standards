## 🎯 Our Reference Intent

References are the "fast-track" to unblocking yourself. Our objective is to provide high-signal, low-noise information that you'll use daily: from choosing the right issue label to executing a complex git hotfix.

- **Precision**: Exact commands and definitions to avoid ambiguity.
- **Speed**: Optimized for rapid scan-ability and copy-paste usage.
- **Consistency**: Unified terminology across all documentation categories.
