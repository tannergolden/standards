## 🎯 Our Operations Intent

Operations is about the long-term sustainability of the project. Our objective is to reduce operational overhead through automation and strict security protocols, ensuring that the repository remains healthy, dependency drift is minimized, and secrets are never exposed.

- **Security**: Branch protections and secret management are non-negotiable.
- **Maintenance**: Regular hygiene tasks prevent stale branches and technical debt.
- **Recovery**: Documented troubleshooting paths ensure rapid resolution of incidents.
