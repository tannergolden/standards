## 🎯 Our Distribution Intent

We believe that the quality of the system is a byproduct of the quality of the workflow. Our objective is to provide a frictionless, automated pipeline that validates every change and delivers value safely and predictably.

- **Automation**: CI/CD pipelines handle the heavy lifting of validation and promotion.
- **Traceability**: Conventional commits and semantic versioning power our release notes.
- **Safety**: Multi-layered testing ensures that regressions are caught before they reach users.
